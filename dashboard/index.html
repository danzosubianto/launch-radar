The category multiple is not a constant: it is the median market-cap/TVL of the tokenized protocols in that category in this snapshot, shrunk toward the global median by peer count. No protocol name is special-cased anywhere in the model.

A protocol “records no token” when its DefiLlama record carries no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol. That is a statement about the record, not the world: a protocol may still have a token its record does not carry. Derivative rows are wrapped, staked or bridged products of tokens that already trade — their value sits with the parent token, so the model prices them at a fraction and their farm plan says so.

These protocols already trade a token, so the radar does not rank them: it shows the market's own value. They are also the evidence base — their market-cap/TVL ratios are what the board's category multiples are measured from.
